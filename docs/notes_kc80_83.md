## kc80: the room marked LAUNDRY

with `kdSA()` on or off. In the `gone` band Misaki's spoken lines are replaced by what the hands do.

  You can use this one”).

  barred himself from the room.

## kc82: one question a day

- **Ask him / Ask them one thing** in the house menu: twelve questions each way (eight here, four from kc83), asked in
  order, answered straight (`e2lAskd`). “Would you have done it, in the trailer?” – “Yes… I would have been wrong, and I
  would never have found it out.” They get the truth out of each other on everything except the one question, which
  is why the corridor hurts.
- Location scenes: Ronin and the back pew; Angel and the reflector (“you’ve put yourself on a four-step leash”); the
  hounds lying down halfway between them. Threads: Angel (“in love or scared”), Ronin (days nobody died). A morning:
  “thank you”, said asleep, through the wall.
