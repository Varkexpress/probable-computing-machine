## The direction (author)

“add some more delusion angst, taking the characterization from the provided text samples”.

## How the samples are used

`docs/voice_sample.md` has one short piece per character. Each gave its working voice, and here those voices are
turned on the infirmary.
- **Misaki** (“Bloodbath”): “Oh, fuck. Misaki’s really gone and done it this time”; “et cetera, whatever”; “Life
  can be so unfair”; the job counted out (“One… Two… Three, and this they will believe wholeheartedly”); the fear
  that runs “bam-bam-bam” under it; “This is no different”; “sugar honey dumpling”; “(They have to.)”. In the
  sample Misaki is the one who comes and undoes the knots. Here nobody is coming to undo theirs.
- **V** (“Waste Not, Want Not”): the table, “merely made of metal… crude, but serviceable”; “Justice,” with a smile
  that twitches; “I find that your kind say that about me, often”; “Now, shall we get to work?”; a fine platter for
  his animals; some deaths must be long-suffering. Misaki has heard all of these about him, and in the delusion they
  land on Misaki. V hears himself say them out of habit.
- **Angel** (“Deliverance”): “None of them can say they didn’t have it coming”; the livestream, asking for leads.
- **Ronin** (“Reindeer Games”): “This one’s mine to kill.”

The standing rule holds: in the cell Misaki takes the tall one for K9, who has bought them, and never warms to him.
