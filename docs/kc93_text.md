### What you wanted  (p_power · V · either setting)

*You open the file at the first page, where the aims are. You wrote them in April.*

*THAT THEY SHOULD KNOW WHAT IS COMING. THAT THEY SHOULD FEAR IT. THAT THEY SHOULD UNDERSTAND WHY.*

*The first two have been met in full. They know what is coming; you sent it to their telephone. They fear it so completely that they will sit up on broken ribs at two words from you.*

*You had imagined it differently. You had imagined a killer brought to see what they had done: sullen, and afraid, and at last serious. You had wanted, you think, to be taken seriously by them.*

**V:** (This is what fear is, when it is finished. It is not seriousness. It is somebody asking whether they have been good.)

*It has no understanding in it. It cannot have. The third aim wants a mind, and the first two take the mind away. You wrote three lines that cannot all be had of one person, and you did not see it, because you had only ever watched from your side of the table.*

**V:** (Every one of the forty-one knew what was coming, and feared it. I told each of them why. I do not now believe that a single one of them was able to hear me.)

*You sit with that. It is eleven years. It is the whole of the book. You had thought that you were teaching something, and you were only frightening things until they stopped.*
