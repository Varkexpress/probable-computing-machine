# The drip (kc91)

`src/kc91.js`; tests `tools/k91_test.js` (45 checks) and `tools/k86_test.js` (the pool); reading copy
`docs/kc91_text.md` (`node tools/room_dump.js 91`). Needs kc73 and kc76–kc90. About 5,000 words.

## The direction (author)

Make it clear that during the delusion (the cell stage of the infirmary) Misaki is on large amounts of medicinal
drugs, and that this contributes to it.

## How it fits what was already canon

kc28 has the chart in V's hand: NO SEDATIVES, underlined twice. The captors dosed Misaki in the other building. Both
stand. What kc91 adds:

- Three broken ribs, a pinned hand and wrists open to the tendon need a great deal of pain relief, and what the
  captors gave is still leaving the blood. Nobody sedates them. They are full of painkiller all the same, by a line
  in the arm, and at that quantity it does to their judgement what a sedative would.
- It tastes exactly as the other building's doses tasted. For Misaki that settles who has them: “same stuff. same
  people. they’ve just moved me somewhere with a nicer ceiling.” The drug is why the room will not hold still, why
  pieces go missing from the afternoon, why there are two of him, why his voice arrives late and slowed and could be
  anybody's, and why they cannot get to three.
- V forbade sedation so that they would be clear, and then had to choose between their pain and their reason. “While
  it runs, they do not hurt. While it runs, I am whatever that building left in their head. I may have one or the
  other.”

No drug is named and no dose or quantity is given (the test checks the source). In the full-story setting the doctor
sets the rate; in injuries only V sets it himself from the tables he uses for the hounds (kc36: no doctor).

## New facts to confirm with the author

- The infirmary pain relief is by drip; it and the captors' residue are a cause of the delusion, with fear and the
  room itself.
- V halves the rate on the first night, or does not (player's choice); Misaki may kink the line.
- V records the hour of every visit to the room in the green book (kc90's `h_log`), so that there is something to
  check that does not require asking him.
