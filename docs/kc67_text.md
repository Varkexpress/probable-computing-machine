### vr67\_church

*He sings every hymn without the book, in a voice you did not know he owned. *In all the years you have kept his file, you have never seen him quiet in a church.*

*He buttons the slip into his shirt pocket. On the steps outside he makes you eat a communion wafer he stole from the sacristy, to see if you go up in flames.
