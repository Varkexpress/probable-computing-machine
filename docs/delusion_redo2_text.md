The base scenes, the nights and the older-format scenes were also redone and are listed in docs/notes_kc96.md; they are best read in the game. Misaki's lines show the fuller tier.

# Part one: by setting

### Where you sleep  (x2_corner · Misaki · full story · cell)

*You do not sleep lying down. You sleep sitting up, in the corner where the bed meets the wall, with the blanket round you and your knees up, so that there is wall on two sides and nothing behind you.*

*It is bad for the ribs. You know that. You have done the sum, and the ribs lost.*

*The tall one comes in at six and finds you like that. He stands there for a while. Then he goes away, and comes back with three more pillows, and puts them on the foot of the bed where you can reach them, and goes out again.*

**Misaki:** (pillows. for the goose. …no. hang on. three pillows is cover. he has just handed me cover)

*You build the corner up with them, as you would build a hide. From behind three pillows, with the spoon, you could make him pay for the last metre.*

**Misaki:** (either he has never been in a fight, or he doesn’t think that i am one. i’ll show him which)

*It is also much more comfortable. You hate that.*

### Which way you lie  (x2_back · Misaki · full story · cell)

### The door, when he is in the room  (x2_gap · Misaki · full story · cell)

### Breathe properly  (y2_breathe · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

*Broken ribs, you breathe deep, ten times, every hour, however much it hurts. If you breathe shallow to spare them, the lung fills, and then you have a real problem.*

*Nobody told you that in here. You have known it since you were about eight.*

*So every hour, by the blinks of the green light, you sit up and do ten. It is like being stabbed, slowly, by someone polite.*

**Misaki:** (seven… eight… fuck… nine… fuck, fuck… ten. gold star)

*On the third round the tall one is in the doorway, with a sheet of paper in his hand. It has a drawing of a pair of lungs on it. He has come to teach you something, and he has found you doing it already.*

*He folds the paper in half, and then in half again, and puts it in his pocket. You give him the look that you give clients who explain rifles to you.*

### A bit of tooth  (y2_tooth · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

*A piece of the back tooth comes away in the soup. You find it with your tongue. It is about the size of a grain of rice.*

*You do not spit it out. He has a file on you with a page for what you eat. He is not getting a page for your teeth.*

**Misaki:** (keep it. if he doesn’t know that it’s gone, he can’t write it down)

*You hold it under your tongue through the rest of the bowl. When he comes for the tray you have it in your fist, under the blanket.*

*He looks at the fist. He looks at your jaw, where you are holding it wrong. He says that he would like to see what is in your hand, and that nothing will follow from it.*

*You open the hand. You keep your eyes on his. You have decided that if he reaches for your mouth, he loses a finger.*

*He does not reach. He takes the piece of tooth between finger and thumb, and holds it under the light. Then he wraps it in his handkerchief, a linen one with a letter in the corner, and puts it in his breast pocket, and goes out.*

**Misaki:** (he took it. he’s keeping it. …trophies. he’s a trophy guy. i knew it. all that “justice,” and he has a drawer)

### You are going to sneeze  (y2_sneeze · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

**Misaki:** (no. no, no, no. not now. think about bricks. think about bricks)

### Nobody has done the fingers  (y3_splint · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

*Nobody has done the fingers. If they are left, they will set bent, and a bent trigger finger is the end of rent.*

*So you do them, in the night, by the green light, with your back to the door. The spoon off the first tray. A strip off the hem of the sheet, torn with your teeth. Two fingers bound to the handle, straight, with the knot on the back.*

*It takes an hour. You stop every time there is a step in the corridor and hide the hand under your thigh.*

**Misaki:** (if he finds it he’ll take the spoon. if he takes the spoon they set bent. so he doesn’t find it)

*He finds it at six. He lifts the blanket to see to the drip, and there it is, and he stands looking down at a spoon tied to a hand.*

*You wait for him to take it. You have the fist ready, such as it is. That is your trigger finger, and he is not having it set bent.*

*He lays the blanket back over the hand, exactly as it was, and goes out. When he comes back he has a proper splint, padded, and he puts it on the counter where you can reach it, and leaves you to change them over yourself. He does not touch the spoon. His face is the colour of the tile. He has just splinted the finger that you mean to shoot him with, and you cannot tell whether he has worked that out.*

### Out of ten  (y3_number · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

*He asks you to put a number to the pain, out of ten.*

*It is a trap, and an old one. Say high and they know where to press. Say low and they think you have something left.*

**Misaki:** (what’s the number that makes him stop asking?)

**Misaki:** two

*It is also true, as it happens. The hand is three bones and the ribs are three more, and next to some things you have had, it is a two. You did not mean to tell him the truth. It was simply the safest number.*

*He writes it down. He looks at the hand, and at the number, and at you. You watch him work out what your ten must have been, and you see the exact moment that he gets there.*

**Misaki:** (don’t look at me like that. that’s not on the menu. you get the hand and the ribs)

*He leaves two tablets on the counter and does not say what they are. You do not take them. You have never been anywhere that it paid to be slow.*

### There is blood when you go  (y3_blood · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

*There is blood in it when you go. Pink, in the steel pan, like water somebody has rinsed a brush in.*

*You know what that is. A kidney took a boot. Pink is a bruise; red is a split. It clears in three days if you drink. You have had it twice, and the first time, at eleven, you thought that you were dying and told nobody, because whoever you told would have had to pay for a doctor.*

*He will see the pan. He sees everything. So either he sees it and you said nothing, or you say.*

**Misaki:** there’s blood. it’s the kidney. it stops if i get water

*You say it flat, as you would report damage on a truck. You are asking the man who means to kill you for a glass of water, so that you will be in better condition when he does it. You are aware of how that sounds.*

*He brings a jug. He fills the glass himself, and his hand shakes on the jug, and some of it goes on the counter.*

**Misaki:** (he’s shaking. he’s angry about the pan. he wanted me whole)

## Thin (7 and over)

### 1

*You cannot remember why you hate him.*

*You know the facts. A card man, behind a bowling alley. A file. A book with your name in it. You can say them. They are like a grudge that somebody else told you about, a long time ago, at a party.*

**Misaki:** (you need it. you need it. it’s the only thing holding the roof up)

> text-speak tier: (u need it. u NEED it. it’s the only thing holding the roof up)

*You try being angry about the camera in the smoke alarm. It was a good one; it used to work every time. You get a small, tired flicker, like a pilot light, and you sit round it with both hands.*

### 2

*You do not eat. It is not to make a point. You cannot remember the reason that people do it.*

*You think about the trailer. The rent receipt, in your back pocket: PAID. You were so pleased with that receipt. You were going to walk into a bowling alley and slap it down in front of a billionaire and say: see? Somebody like me. On time.*

*You cannot now see why it mattered what he thought. That frightens you more than the bulb does. You have spent since April caring very much what that man thought, and it was the best fun you ever had.*

### 3

*There are his shoes in the passage. Hard soles, coming on, not hurrying.*

*Nothing gets up. That is new. Every time until now your whole body has stood to: the spoon, the list, the count of his steps. You wait for it.*

**Misaki:** (four steps. …so? four steps. and?)

> text-speak tier: (four steps. …so? four steps. and?)

*He comes in with the tray. He looks at you, and at your hands, which are lying open on the blanket with nothing in them. You have had some bad looks from him. You have never had that one. He looks like a man who has come to a fight and found the other side gone home.*

## Gone

### 1

*You do not do the inventory. There is no point counting things.*

*You lie on your side facing the wall. You have never done that in your life, in any room. You have always faced the door. A person faces the door so as to see it coming, and you find that you do not much want to see it.*

*On the other side of the door the shoes stop. He stands there for a long time. He has noticed which way you are facing. You think, without any heat, that it must be annoying for him: all that law, and nobody left to read it to.*
