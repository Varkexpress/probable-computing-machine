## kc93: the damage itself

and that they go on working in a safe room with nobody applying them. On V's side it is what it does to stand where
the men stood. The text is the same under both aftermath settings.

## The rapport pass

The author asked a second time for less friendliness between the two during the delusion. Every cell-stage scene was
re-read for it, including kc73's original ones.

- **kc73, the dressing** (V's fourth scene, the first module's own text): Misaki used to chat (“you’re good at this…
  it’s a good system. I’d use it”). They now bargain out of dread: “I know what the careful one is for. I’ll talk.
  You don’t have to do the rest.” Their face is empty, and the hand in his is braced.
- **kc85 `y_ties`**: no joke about being good at holding still; “I won’t make it hard.” They do not find him a
  “weird guy”; they ask themselves why he wants them to know what the scissors are for.
- **kc88 `y2_tooth`**: they no longer leave the piece of tooth tidily on the tray and explain. They hide it, because
  goods that arrive short are worth less, and open the fist expecting to learn the cost.
- **kc90, night II (injuries only)**: hiding the wrist under the blanket was written as a kindness to him. It is now
  fear: the sight of them turned his stomach, and goods that disgust the buyer do not get kept. V understands that
  they have taken his sickness at what was done for disgust at what they are.
- **kc93 `p_under`**: the mat is not funny to them; it is so that he knows where they will be.

Rule for future writing: in the cell stage Misaki does not banter with the tall one, thank him warmly, help him, or
find him odd in a fond way. They comply, watch his hands, bargain, test, and wait. Any warmth they feel is a symptom,
and they know it (`p_grateful`).
