# What it costs (kc79)

All in `src/kc79.js`; tested by `tools/k79_test.js`; reading copy in `docs/kc79_text.md`. Needs kc73, kc76, kc77, kc78.

The author asked for more content, true to the characters, with more angst, pain and tragedy. `docs/voice_sample.md`
was the reference: Misaki talks themself through fear in numbered steps with the terror underneath, and here never
reaches three; “life is so fucking unfair”. V states things as fact (“merely”, “a wanton thing”, “aberration”), and the
true thought is in brackets. Neither of them explains a feeling in full. Nothing names or shows what happened in the
hours.
