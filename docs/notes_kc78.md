# Not an enemy, and still in danger (kc78)

All in `src/kc78.js`; tested by `tools/k78_test.js`; reading copy in `docs/kc78_text.md`. Needs kc73, kc76, kc77.

## The direction (author)

The route needed a real conflict in its middle. The author asked for it to come when the two of them realise they do
not consider the other an enemy, because each believes the other would still kill them without a second thought given
the chance.

So each of them has the realisation alone, and in the same minute is certain of the other:

- **Misaki** finds they have spent nine minutes keeping V alive through a scope. Then: his book is in ink, he does not
  cross things out, he told them so. “He’ll do it on the day it says, between the tea and the hounds, and feel nothing.”
- **V** finds he stood with his back to their rifle and felt covered. Then: they have never withdrawn the car park, and
  they kill for whoever pays. “On the day somebody prices me, they will do it between two mouthfuls of cereal and never
  ask what I had done.” His original charge against them (wanton, no law) is now what he is afraid of.

Each one's evidence is the other's own words from chapter I, which neither has taken back. Every kindness since then
can be read as patience (his) or as work (theirs). Neither can say it first, because saying it first means disarming
in front of somebody you believe would do it. That is the conflict; it is not a misunderstanding either of them can
clear up by being nicer.
