# Out of the infirmary: enemies under one roof

## Misaki, to V

### Take his glasses off his face

*He is at the desk with the ledger. You walk up behind him, loudly, so that it is fair, and lift his reading glasses off his nose.*

*He does not grab. He sits perfectly still, with the pen above the page, looking at nothing.*

**V:** You are standing eleven inches to my left. You smell of my soap.

*You put them on. The room goes to soup. The ledger is a grey field with lines in it.*

**Misaki:** how do you see?

> text-speak tier: how do u SEE

**V:** I do not, without them. You now hold the one thing that would give you a fair fight. I suggest that you use it or return it.

*You put them back on his face, crooked. He leaves them crooked for the rest of the evening, and writes four lines like that, and they slope.*

*(again)*

*You take the glasses again. He waits, with the pen up, and you count to thirty before you give them back. He has not moved by a hair. You are fairly sure that he was counting too.*

### Pat him down

*You tell him to put his arms out. He does it at once, like a man at an airport, and looks at the ceiling while you go down him.*

*Collar, left side: the scalpel, where it always is. Inside pocket: a fountain pen, a list of your doses in his hand, and two dog biscuits.*

**Misaki:** you carry dog biscuits?

> text-speak tier: u carry DOG BISCUITS

**V:** I carry what is needed.

*You leave the scalpel where it is. You keep one biscuit, for spite. He watches you put it in your pocket and says nothing at all, which is how you know it was for the old hound.*

*(again)*

*You pat him down. Scalpel, pen, three biscuits today. He is carrying one extra. You do not ask who for.*

### Put your boots on his desk

*You sit in the good chair in his study and put both boots up on the desk, crossed, on the open ledger.*

*He comes in. He looks at the boots, and at the ledger under them. He lifts the ledger out from under your heels, very carefully, and moves it eight inches to the left, and sits down to write in it there.*

**Misaki:** (he moved the book. not the boots. the book)

> text-speak tier: (he moved the BOOK. not the boots. the book)

*You stay for an hour out of principle. He writes the whole time and never once tells you to go, and it is the worst hour of your week.*

*(again)*

*Boots on the desk. He moves the ledger eight inches. You move your boots eight inches. He moves it back. You do this until the hall clock strikes, and neither of you laughs, and neither of you stops.*

### Fall asleep where he can see you

*It is a test. You lie down on the couch in the big room, with your back to him and the spoon in your fist, and you shut your eyes, and you breathe slow.*

*An hour. Nothing comes. You open one eye. He is in the armchair with a book, the old hound across his feet. He has not moved. He has not looked up.*

*There is a coat over you. You did not hear it arrive.*

**Misaki:** (ok. test failed. i don’t know whose. his coat smells of dogs and money)

> text-speak tier: (ok. test failed. i don’t know whose. his coat smells like dogs and money)

*You keep your eyes shut until he goes up. Then you keep the coat.*

*(again)*

*You try it again. This time you actually sleep. When you wake the coat is there, and the book is shut on his knee, and he is looking at the door, not at you, as if he were the one on watch.*

### Throw something at him

*You throw an apple at his head from across the kitchen. It goes an inch past his ear and bursts on the tiles behind him.*

**V:** An inch.

**Misaki:** i can do closer

*You throw the second one closer. He catches it without looking up from the paper, and eats it.*

**Misaki:** (that’s cheating. you can’t catch the warning shot. that isn’t how warnings work)

> text-speak tier: (that’s cheating. u can’t catch the warning shot. that’s not how warnings WORK)

*(again)*

*You throw a satsuma. He catches it, peels it, and puts half of it in front of you without a word. You eat it. You will deny this under any circumstances.*

### Sit at the other end of the couch

> text-speak tier: this film’s shit

**V:** It is.

*(again)*

### Leave the hall light on

*They get up at night and go along the passage to the bathroom. You leave the hall light on, low, so that they will not walk into the dark.*

*At one in the morning you hear them stop under it. The switch goes. Off.*

*You get up and put it on. At two it is off again. At three you find a piece of tape over the switch, holding it down, and a note on the tape in biro: “i’m not 5”.*

**V:** (They are not five. At five they would have let me.)

*You leave the tape. You put a lamp on the hall table instead. In the morning it has been turned to face the wall, and it is still on.*

*(again)*

*The lamp in the hall is on. It is turned to the wall. It stays on all night. That is as far as the negotiation is going to get, and you take it.*
