## What it adds

Thirty-four scenes in kc85's player-opened pool (now 242). Pain and the delusion, and what sits beside them: money,
work, friends, a newspaper, a notebook, and a man from the river with a daughter.

Misaki, while the room is a cell (they take the tall one for K9, who means to kill them; they never warm to him):
`w_ribs` breathing in quarters, timed to his steps · `w_itch` under the cast, and the spoon · `w_tooth` the broken
edge · `w_pump` the beep, and listening for him · `w_faces` the four, losing their faces · `w_tremor` the good hand
shakes · `w_ear` the left ear, and his kindness · `w_clutch` the truck, in a dream · `w_nails` waiting for him to
press · `w_ankle` on the floor until morning · `w_paper` four lines on page seven · `w_straw` they will not drink
from his hand · `w_lullaby` a tune from before nine.

Misaki, once clear: `w_cost` what a hospital would have cost · `w_photo` last summer, ten fingers · `w_client` a
job they cannot answer · `w_stairs` fourteen steps, no help · `w_taste` nothing tastes · `w_wake` the door left
open, so they can see him not come in · `w_angelmsg` all two hundred and eleven of Angel's messages · `w_lace` the
elastic laces.

V: `w_pumpV` the minute between the beep and the bag · `w_earV` he comes in on the right side, and books a
specialist · `w_itchV` a sanded beech stick, binned and then used · `w_ronin` “I asked how bad.” · `w_school` the
driver's daughter is in the Tuesday girl's class · `w_stairsV` his nails in his palms · `w_angel` “I do not know.”
· `w_nailsV` they wait for him to press · `w_ankleV` he sits on the floor at their level · `w_paperV` he burns the
newspapers · `w_bookV` FOR FUN, a problem stopped halfway · `w_laceV` elastic, threaded at night · `w_facesV` a
card under the door for the fourth man.

The humanizing pass: both scanners clean, then a hand pass over every scene that cut summarised feelings and tidy
closing lines (about twenty endings now stop on an action).
