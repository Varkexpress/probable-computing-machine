## What it adds

who holds them and means to kill them. They never warm to him; the anger seethes. Checked with both scanners and by
hand for summarised feelings and tidy closers.

Misaki: `n_smell` he is too well brought up to notice the smell · `n_hair` hair on the pillow, kept under the
mattress · `n_dream` the bowling alley, and waking from it · `n_clock` the time he says, and their own count by
thumbnail · `n_pray` at three in the morning they try to hire God · `n_total` what the total on their page will be ·
`n_good` “Good.” · `n_fast` they stop eating to see what he does · `n_dark` the power goes; a candle in the doorway,
held up by his face · `n_moth` a moth at the light all night.

V: `n_hairV` he lets them keep it · `n_goodV` he stops saying the word, even to the hounds · `n_fastV` three days
with the bowl on his knee, and the tube in the drawer · `n_darkV` the candle, from his side · `n_clockV` a clock and a
calendar they cannot believe.
