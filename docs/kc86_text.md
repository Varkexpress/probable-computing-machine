### Work out which way is north  (g_north · Misaki · either setting · cell)

*There is no window. You work out north anyway, because a room you cannot point out of is a room you cannot leave.*

*The pipe along the ceiling is warm at one end and cold at the other, so the boiler is that way. The drain slopes to the door. The tall one’s shoes are wet on some days, and only on the left side, which gives you the wind.*

**Misaki:** (north is the corner with the drip stand. probably. sixty-forty)

*It takes you most of a morning. When he comes in with the tray, you are facing north, and he stops, because you are smiling at a wall.*

**Misaki:** (don’t ask. you wouldn’t get it. you’ve got windows)

### There is a spoon on the tray  (g_spoon · Misaki · either setting · cell)

*The spoon on the tray is metal. In the other building they were plastic, and counted.*

**Misaki:** (metal. he’s left me metal. that’s not a mistake. they don’t make mistakes)

*You could do a great deal with a metal spoon and a week. You put it down the side of the mattress and wait for him to count the tray.*

*He does not count the tray. The next one comes with a spoon, and a fork, and a knife with a proper edge on it.*

**Misaki:** (okay. it’s a test. nobody’s this dumb. it’s a test, and i don’t know what passing looks like)

*You eat with the knife and fork, very correctly, and put all three back on the tray in a row. You leave the first spoon where it is. You have decided that it is there to be found on you.*

### Your shoe  (g_shoe · Misaki · either setting · cell)

*One of your shoes is on the floor by the bed. The left one. Somebody has cleaned it; the lace has been washed.*

*You do not know where the right one is. You think about that for a while. A person with one shoe is not going anywhere, and whoever put it there knows it.*

**Misaki:** (one shoe. so that i know i’m not walking out. got it)

*That evening the tall one comes in with a shoe box. There is a pair in it, new, your size, the same make. He puts the box under the bed, next to the single shoe, and says nothing about either.*

**Misaki:** (…so now i’ve got three. what do three shoes mean?)

### He says your name  (g_number · Misaki · either setting · cell)

*He has not called you anything. Then, on the second day, he says your name.*

*Nobody in the other building had it. You were careful. You were “it” to one of them and “sweetheart” to another, and you kept the name back the whole time, behind your teeth, like the round in the shoe.*

*So he has had it from somewhere else. There is a file. Somebody has sold him the one thing you got out of there with.*

**Misaki:** (don’t answer to it. if you answer to it, it’s his)

*You look at his hands and say nothing. He says it again, lower. It is worse, lower.*

*He does not try a third time. For the rest of the day he calls you nothing, and your own name stays in the room, like something he has put down on the counter and may pick up again.*

### The door handle  (g_handle · Misaki · either setting · cell)

*You have not tried the door. You do not try doors in here. Trying a door tells them that you still think about doors.*

*But you watch it. When the tall one goes out, he pulls it shut, and there is no second sound after the latch. No bolt. No key.*

**Misaki:** (so it’s electric. or there’s a guy outside. or it’s just open)

**Misaki:** (it’s not open. don’t be stupid. nobody leaves it open)

*You lie on your right side and look at the handle for an hour. It is an ordinary brushed steel handle. You are more afraid of it than you were of the men.*

### Practise the good voice  (g_voice · Misaki · either setting · cell)

*You practise it when nobody is in the room. It has to be ready.*

**Misaki:** thank you. yes, please. no trouble. whatever’s easiest

*It is pitched a little higher than yours, with the swearing taken out. You found it in about a day, in the other building. It turned out you had always had it.*

**Misaki:** (you’re so good at this. it’s disgusting. you should’ve been in the drama club)

*Through the wall, something heavy is set down, hard, on stone. Then nothing. You do the voice again, quieter, in case that was about you.*

### The green light on the ceiling  (g_eye · Misaki · either setting · cell)

*There is a white disc on the ceiling with a green light in it. It blinks every nine seconds.*

*It is a camera. Of course it is. So everything you do in this bed, you do for whoever is on the other end of it.*

*You perform sleeping. You are good at it: slow breath, slack mouth, the hand open. You do it for six hours with your eyes shut, wide awake, counting blinks through your eyelids.*

**Misaki:** (two thousand four hundred. he’s watched me not sleep two thousand four hundred times. enjoy the show, creep)

*It is a smoke detector. You will find that out on the third day, and you will want those six hours back more than anything else you lost that week.*

### The sign on the door  (g_sign · Misaki · either setting · clear)

*You can read the door from the bed now, backwards, through the glass panel. YRDNUAL.*

**Misaki:** he put me in the laundry. i thought i was being murdered, and i was in the laundry

*You say it to the ceiling. Then you say it again, because it is the first sentence in a week that has come out in your own voice, and you want to hear what that sounds like.*

**Misaki:** grandpa! hey, grandpa! your sign’s stupid!

*Nobody answers. Somewhere a long way up the house a door closes, carefully. You find that you are grinning, and that it hurts your face, as if the muscles had been put away for the season.*

### Ronin, with a chair  (g_ronin · Misaki · either setting · clear)

*Ronin comes in with a kitchen chair and puts it down backwards and sits on it. He has brought the crowbar. He always brings the crowbar.*

**Ronin:** You look like shit. I’ve dug up better.

**Misaki:** thanks. you smell like a church

**Ronin:** ’Cause I been in one. Lit a candle for you. Then I lit one for whoever I’m gonna find, so it’s even.

*He tells you a story about a priest, a goat and a collection plate that you could not repeat to anybody. You laugh, and the ribs catch, and you laugh at that too.*

**Ronin:** There it is. Thought he’d fuckin’ broke it.

**Misaki:** who, v? he didn’t do this

**Ronin:** I know he didn’t. I checked. Sat outside this door four nights with a crowbar, checkin’.

*He says it as if it was nothing. You look at the crowbar, and at the door, and you understand who the chair in the corridor was for.*

### The tray  (g_kneel · V · either setting)

*You are a tall man. In that room, with them flat on the bed, you are the height of the door.*

*So you kneel to put the tray down. You have not knelt to anybody in your life. You do it four times a day, on the tile, in a good suit.*

**V:** (It is not humility. It is a matter of sight lines. A thing looked at from below is a larger thing.)

*They thank you. They thank the top of your head, since that is what they have been given.*

**V:** (I am on my knees to a person whose name is in my book under TO BE KILLED. I have looked for the contradiction, and I find that I am not interested in it.)

### Your shoes  (g_soles · V · either setting)

*On the second day you go down in the leather-soled pair, the ones that ring on stone, and you put your feet down hard the whole length of the kennel corridor.*

**V:** (They should hear me from the stair. Nobody ought to arrive at that door without warning. I, least of all.)

*You hear the breathing in the room change when your step reaches the corner. They are bracing. You have given them twelve seconds in which to do it.*

**V:** (It is a wretched gift. It is what I have.)

### Inspector Hale rings  (g_hale · V · either setting)

*Inspector Hale rings at nine. A trailer in lot fourteen has been standing open for days. The tenant is known to him. He would like to know whether the tenant is known to you.*

**V:** By reputation.

*He says that there was blood in a building on the river, and a great deal of it was not the tenant’s. He says that he is not asking who did that.*

**V:** Then I have nothing to tell you, Inspector.

*He waits. He is good at it; it is why you have never underrated him.*

**V:** If I had seen them, they would be in a bed, and in no state to be questioned. I would say the same of anybody.

*He thanks you and rings off. In eleven years you have not told that man one lie. You have now told him the truth in such a way that it will do the work of one.*

**V:** (I had a letter to him, with their thumbprint in it. I have just stood between them and him. I will take the letter out of the safe tonight.)

### Feeding time  (g_meat · V · either setting)

*You bring the steel bucket down to the kennel at six, as you do every evening.*

*The hounds do not come to it. They stand in the passage, all four, with their heads turned towards the door at the far end, the one marked LAUNDRY.*

**V:** Eat.

*The black hound looks at the bucket and then at you. It has never in its life had to be told twice.*

**V:** (They can smell them. Blood, and that particular fear. They have every one of them smelled like that, once, on my table.)

*You put the bucket down and leave it. In the morning it is empty, and all four are asleep against the infirmary door in a heap, and you step over them to take in the tray.*

### Read the page aloud  (g_aloud · V · either setting)

*You take the ledger down to the cold room, where nobody will hear, and you read their page aloud. You do this with every page, before a thing is done. It is a way of being sure.*

**V:** “Misaki. Wanton. Collateral: several.”

*Your voice is quite steady. The driver, the doorman, the man on a Tuesday. You read them all.*

**V:** “To be —”

*You try it again. You have said that word over forty-one tables. It is one of the commonest words in your mouth.*

**V:** (I have a full sentence, written in my own hand, that I am not able to say.)

*You close the book. The steel table is beside you, with the straps gone from it. You had not noticed until now that you were standing with your hand on it, as on the shoulder of something that had been put down.*
