### e2l\_night

**V:** I am outside. I am not coming in. There are two bolts between us. My coat is on the bonnet of the car, where you can see it from the window, and the scalpel is in the pocket.

**V:** You are in your trailer. It is Tuesday. The door opens from your side.

*You wake at three in the trailer, and it is not the trailer. It is the room with the one bulb. The bolts are on the wrong side of the door, and your left hand has shut and will not open.*

**Misaki:** (One: this is the trailer. Two: this is the trailer; look, there’s the cactus, the stupid cactus. Three—)

*You do not get to three. Your right hand has the phone, and it has rung the only number it knows without asking you.*

**V:** (on the phone) Misaki.

*You cannot make a word. He listens to you breathe for about four seconds, and then you hear car keys.*

*Eleven minutes later there are headlights in the lot, and then his voice on the other side of your door. He does not touch the handle.*

*Your phone rings at three. It is Misaki. They do not speak. You listen to them breathe for four seconds. You know the sound: you heard it through a vent for a week.*

*You are in the car before you have decided anything. In the trailer lot you take off your coat and leave it on the bonnet, with the scalpel in the pocket, where it can be seen from their window.*

**V:** (I am the thing they bolt this door against. I am also the number they rang. I cannot tell which of us that is worse for.)

*You stand four steps back from the door, in the rain, and do not touch the handle.*

**Misaki:** (He’s who I’m scared of, and he’s who I called. What the fuck is wrong with me?)

*Inside, something small falls over. Then nothing, for a long time, except their breathing, which you can hear through the door.*

*It takes both hands, and a long time. He is standing four steps back in his shirtsleeves in the rain, with his arms held out from his sides, soaked to the skin. He looks at your left hand before he looks at your face.*

*(or)* *It takes them a long time. When the door opens you are still four steps back, in your shirtsleeves, with your arms held out from your sides. You look at the left hand before you look at their face.*

**V:** May I?

*You hold it out. He opens your fingers one at a time, as he did when he changed the dressing, and comes not one step nearer than he has to.*

*(or)* *They hold it out. You open the fingers one at a time, as you did when you changed the dressing, and go not one step nearer than you have to.*

**Misaki:** This doesn’t change anything, V. I want that understood.

**V:** No. I will be gone before it is light, and tomorrow you may go back to believing whatever keeps you alive.

**Misaki:** Don’t come in. Please don’t come in.

**V:** I will not.

*He does not. You hear him sit down on the step, in the rain. Every so often he tells you the day, and that the door opens from your side, in the voice he uses for reading out minutes.*

*(or)* *You sit down on their step, in the rain. Every so often you tell the door what day it is, and that it opens from their side. You use the voice in which you read out minutes, because it is the only one you have that does not shake.*

*At some point your hand opens. When it gets light the step is empty and wet, except for one dry patch the width of a man.*

*(or)* *Towards five the breathing behind the door evens out. You go before it is light, as they would wish. You leave a dry patch on the step, and you find that you resent the rain for the speed at which it will cover it.*
