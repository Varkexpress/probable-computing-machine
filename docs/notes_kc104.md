## What it adds

Thirteen scenes in kc85's player-opened pool (now 208), all set while Misaki believes that the tall one is K9, who
holds them and means to kill them. The standing rule holds: they never warm to him. The anger seethes (kc102). What
these add is what the cell takes slowly: a skill, a date, a song, a way of seeing out. Several come in pairs, one from
each side of the door.

Misaki:
- `t_finger` They practise the trigger pull in their head all night, and lose whether you breathe out before the shot.
- `t_march` The enrolment form says “March”. It is the fourteenth. They eat the soup as if it were the cake.
- `t_window` They scratch a window on the wall and put the laundromat roof in it. He looks at it, and it goes out.
- `t_hymn` They hum Ronin's hymn to keep time. Somebody in the passage hums the next line, in tune.
- `t_wind` The drip eats the eight-hundred-metre wind table. They fill the space with his hands.
- `t_text` A message to Angel, in their head, rewritten for days: whether to sign it.
- `t_chair` He falls asleep in the corner chair, three steps away. They have the spoon, and do not, and cannot work
  out why.
- `t_peach` A fresh peach on the fourteenth of March. They do not eat it.

V:

- `t_windowV` He leaves two doors open so that the landing's light falls on the scratched window at six, for nine
  minutes, every morning.
- `t_marchV` The enrolment form, the first peach of the season, eaten by him at the sink. “Possibly.”
- `t_phone` Angel sends their phone two hundred and eleven messages. He charges it and never looks.
