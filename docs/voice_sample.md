# Author's voice sample (canon reference; ignore the second-person reader)

## Deliverance [@Angelic]

"Now, this is a little different from what I usually do on this channel, but this... has been on my mind for the past day," Maria murmurs. Her fans are already filling up the comments of her livestream: red hearts and shocked emojis and 'what happened omfg?' tunnel up her screen.
"As you well know, Elysium City has a serial killer problem." That much is an understatement, between the active serials in the Slaughterhouse Losers to the dozen more untraced. (And yes, well, she is part of the problem, but for a good cause. None of the victims of the Heartsick Angel can say they didn't have it coming.)
But today Maria's only concerned with... one.
"What do you all know of The Kidnapper?"
She exhales and closes her eyes. It feels far too vulnerable, being laid so bare to her audience. To them, she is Maria de la Rosa; and now, she is Maria, exposing a hint of herself as The Heartsick Angel. (And it's odd to say, but there's a sort of thrill in that, too.)
"I'd appreciate it if you could give me any leads." She tries for a smile, because her channel has only seen her so happy: it quivers on her lips. "The Kidnapper has taken someone incredibly important to me. So anything you can give me will be so helpful."
The tips pour in by the minute. It's certainly far more effective than anything the police of Elysium City could've offered her. Not that they were ever effective in the first place. Her viewers mention sightings: a man lurking, dark and haunting, his black hoodie covering up the web of scars on his face. They tell her about her writer, her sweetheart, where they were last seen, where they had last been, and it terrifies her as it reassures her.
(Her lover's alive. They have to be.)
No one who values their life should fuck with her. The Heartsick Angel smiles as she touches up her hair and plucks the shotgun off her shelf. No one is messing with her. No one's messing with her lover.
And The Kidnapper doesn't stand half a chance when Angel executes him with her shotgun, a bloom of red out of his pink throat. (He was screaming, only thirty minutes prior: begging her to stop, don't play this like it is sport, stalking him through his warehouse like predator and prey.)
(But even angels are ravenous: after only too much worry, they need a little catharsis.)
She's an angel: of course she'll save you. Of course she'll kiss you, her fingers wrapped up in yours. Of course you'll taste the blood-smear on her lips; of course you'll giggle.
(Of course. You're in love.)

## Reindeer Games [@goreboy] (Ronin)

You keep accidentally running into serial killers. It's a problem.
Unfortunately for you, you've run into a serial kidnapper-cum-(laude?)-serial killer. Do serial kidnappers deserve the distinction when they graduate to murder?
The Kidnapper wants ransom. He's calling anyone who will listen. And, unfortunately for them, your boyfriend's the first of your contacts. And he happens to be a serial killer, too. (A far more predatory one, at that.)
"You really don't want to know what will happen to you," you murmur. And your kidnapper laughs. "What, who'd you think your boyfriend is? The Devil's Butcher?"
(That exactly. But you can't say that, lest you scare this serial off.)
Minutes drag into hours. Hours drag into even more hours.
(Ronin knows something's wrong. When is he coming?)
"Looks like your little boyfriend isn't coming," the Kidnapper snorts. "Which of your fingers should I send him first?"
"Fourth one, please." The Devil's Butcher snickers, clanging his silver skull rings against the metallic door. "Would suck t'not have that one to wrap a ring round."
The Kidnapper starts. And then he's staggering back. "What the fuck," he's whispering, "what the fuck."
"Hey, don't cha look so shocked," Ronin says. "You did tell me to come pick your victim up." He hooks his crowbar around The Kidnapper's neck, drags him towards his grinning maw. "D'you have an address for that finger? Do I need'ta give it to you too, or d'you still don't know who you're messing with?"
The Kidnapper gasps. Wide eyes and frantic, as if the realisation's tolling in his head.
"Purgatory, please and thank you," Ronin grins. "Aw, too choked up to do it? You're fucking boring."
(And closer, so close that you don't hear: "This one's mine to kill.")
And then The Kidnapper's screaming, slammed to the ground, getting his head bludgeoned in, his blood purpling into bruises and prettier colours. He chokes, he gargles, he screeches, he mumbles a hundred dozen things and you feel a smile quirk your lips, too. You've always liked to watch your boyfriend do his work.
The Devil's Butcher laughs like a lion in feast. He glances towards you and the deranged light in his eyes is close to divine.
"What took you so long?"
Ronin snorts. "Oh, y'know, I was caught up in stuff. Hanging 'round graveyards, getting heads cracked open, that kinda shit."
"You're an asshole." You scoff.
"Sucks t'be you." His shit-eating grin's wide as a fiend. "You chose me as your partner in crime, can't blame me for doin' what I'm s'posed to."
"I can so blame you."
"Nah." He drags his crowbar against the metal chair. A hair of anxiety runs up your skin; a want, a cold thrill.
"Are you gonna get me out of this?"
He crosses his arms. Tilts his head. Snickers. "Also nah. You got yourself in this fuckin' mess, the devil's gonna relish it."

## Bloodbath [@hitmeuppp] (Misaki)

Oh, fuck. Misaki's really gone and done it this time.
They've expected all sorts of shit during their assassination stints. Bomb threats, attempted double homicides, chainsaws malfunctioning when they're just about to go down on a throat, et cetera, whatever.
But having your lover held hostage is a new one. How the fuck do they even know? It's not like Misaki was shouting from the rooftops about their lover (though she honestly would, given the chance, because their sugar honey dumpling totally deserves it). And it's not like their debtors know about her lover, either.
Then this must be a massive fucked-up coincidence. Which is really fucked up, seriously! What has their sugar honey dumpling done other than lie about being a serial killer? Like, that doesn't even crack the top ten of the Most Wanted Serial Liars.
Life can be so unfair.
Inhale. Exhale. They can do this. One: it is not their fault that you're stuck, getting lorded over by a weirdo serial kidnapping serial. (It's not easy to believe, but they've got to buy it, focus.) Two: they're perched in the right spot. Off on a rooftop, visibility low (and so that's a little fucked); but she's done this enough to know she's a straight shot. Three, and this they will believe wholeheartedly: the sucker's got it coming.
It's something so small. It's only a matter of saving you. (And then it's not small at all. And then it's your life on the line, and they're fucking terrified, heart-in-throat hammering, bam-bam-bam and it's all fear, what if I lose, what if I don't find you, what if I die, what if you die—)
Assassinations are cut and dry. Go in with a chainsaw, or a rifle, or a bazooka, or anything her clients want. It's straightforward. It's simple. It's muscle memory, at this point. How is this any different?
This is no different.
Their fear dies in their grip. She steels herself.
They'll save you.
(They have to.)
And so take it shot-for-shot: the machine gun throttles the warehouse, the Kidnapper blows to pieces. Misaki goes in, chainsaw first, and chops the long limp body up. (Hey, don't come at her — a double-tap never ever hurts!)
"You're here! Oh my God, babe, what did I say about hide-and-seek?" Misaki gasps, undoing the knots around your wrist. You feel the tremble of their fingers, and the worry on their face melts you. And then you can't help it: you laugh, and then she laughs, and you're kissing each other in the shitty warehouse, and all is good in the world again.

## Waste Not, Want Not [@K9] (V)

His operating table is merely made of metal. It is crude, but serviceable: equipped with sturdy straps and drains. It is all V needs.
"I don't understand," V says, "what possessed you to do such a thing." He teases the cubed meat between his fingers: cured of parasites, cold and fresh. His animals will enjoy this particular piece of flesh.
(It is rhetorical. Of course V understands. The Kidnapper's crimes trail the streets in leaflets and posters and haunt the eyes of mothers and fathers. He is an aberration, a demented thing begging to be slaughtered. It only so happens that this time it is even closer to home.)
"What the fuck do you get out of this?" The Kidnapper splutters. He's gazing at V with eyes wild with terror: no doubt he is staring at his own stomach, pieces of it cubed out. Some of his guts are trailing, as well, but V pays it no mind. He will clean that up when he is finished.
(And he hopes it is soon. His lover is upstairs in bed: shaken, but unharmed. The Kidnapper is lucky that this is so. The fate that befalls him would've been far darker, else, and his suffering, well...)
"Answer me!"
V leans closer. He leans so close that he can see the sweat tremble on The Kidnapper's upper lip.
"Justice," he murmurs, a smile twitching on his lip. And V does mean justice, in the sense of retribution: lex talionis, an eye for an eye. And perhaps there is a perverse pleasure to be taken from this particular death; the death of the thing that kidnapped his beloved, a thing that has caused him so much grief over the past twenty-four hours.
V cannot say he particularly enjoys prolonging a person's suffering. A quick death is merciful, putting a dark thing down: but some do not deserve quick deaths. Some deaths must be long-suffering, parallel to the hurt they have inflicted upon others.
(And there is fine meat that his animals deserve to eat.)
"You're fucking insane."
"I find that your kind say that about me, often. Now," he says, "shall we get to work?"
This butchering takes several hours of work. And there is a fine platter for his animals, pretty cubed pieces stacked upon one another.
