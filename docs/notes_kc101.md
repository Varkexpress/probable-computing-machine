## The direction (author)

“add some more angst and sadness overall, especially during the infirmary/hopeless period.”

## What kind of sadness

The infirmary already has a great deal of fear and horror. This is grief: what goes on outside the room without
them, what will not come back, and, for V, watching the war (the one thing the two of them had) leave the room before
the person does. It stays in the route's voice: Misaki hides it and makes a joke of it where they can; V states it as
fact. In the cell stage Misaki still takes the tall one for K9 and keeps the sadness from him.
