# The four at the door (kc96)

`src/kc96.js`; test `tools/k96_test.js` (26 checks); reading copy `docs/kc96_text.md` (`node tools/room_dump.js 96`).
Needs kc73 and kc76–kc95. About 2,500 words: six interludes.

## The direction (author)

Another infirmary expansion, and make this one unique.

## What is different about it

Every other scene of the stay is told from inside Misaki's head or V's. These six are told from the kennel passage, by
the hounds. They play for either POV and are the same under either aftermath setting. Each opens on its own card (the
hound's name for the scene, “the kennel passage”, and a strip of light where the gap under the door would be).

Rules of the form (the test checks several of them):
- third person, present tense, one hound at a time. No names at all: the grey hound, the black hound, the pale hound,
  the old hound; **the tall one** and **the small one**.
- “The tall one” is what Misaki calls V in the delusion, where it means a captor. To the hounds it is the name of the
  one who cut the collar off. The same two words carry both.
- A hound knows smell, sound, weight, heat and habit. It does not count past “many”, does not know what a book is,
  and has no word for why. It is never wrong about what is in front of it.
- What was done in the other building is never named. A hound smells “the other place” on the small one and knows it,
  because each of them came out of one.

## The six

1. **The grey hound: the door handle.** She knows the small sound through the wall, because she made it once. She
   opens the door and lies along the small one's back, and waits to stop being feared, as the tall one waited for her.
   “Come.” She lifts one ear. (kc92's `k_hound` / `k_houndV`, from her side.)
2. **The black hound: the stair.** The tall one gets up in the old way, and stops at the top of the back stair like
   something at the end of a chain. Two voices to listen for now, on either side of a door. “It is not for him to
   choose.” (kc94's first scene, from the foot of the stair.)
3. **The pale hound: under the bed.** She does not like doors, and approves of the light. Under is good. She goes in
   beside the small one. Three of them on the floor, and nobody making a sound. (kc93's `p_under`.)
4. **All four: the bucket at the far wall.** The one thing in a day that was never different has moved. “Eat… Please.”
   They do not know the word; they know the voice he used over the eleven. The old hound eats so that the others will.
   (kc90's first night.)
5. **The old hound: the tall one comes down at night.** He knows the smell the eleven had on the third night, and it is
   not under this door. The tall one comes down with the steel on him, and the old hound gets up on a bad hip and
   stands in the way. He has known since the first evening that the tall one would not; “but the tall one needs to
   come down the stair and find out, each time. So each time the old hound will get up.” (kc94, and kc83's laugh.)
6. **The old hound: the door opens from the inside.** The small one, upright, makes one short sound on purpose to
   startle. The tall one sets the jug down. The old hound walks in front at the pace of his hip, and the tall one
   follows four steps behind, who always walked ahead. (kc89's `g_walk`.)
