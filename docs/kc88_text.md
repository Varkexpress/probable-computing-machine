### Breathe properly  (y2_breathe · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

*Broken ribs, you breathe deep, ten times, every hour, however much it hurts. If you breathe shallow to spare them, the lung fills, and then you have a real problem.*

*Nobody told you that in here. You have known it since you were about eight.*

*So every hour, by the blinks of the green light, you sit up and do ten. It is like being stabbed, slowly, by someone polite.*

**Misaki:** (seven… eight… fuck… nine… fuck, fuck… ten. gold star)

*On the third round the tall one is in the doorway, with a sheet of paper in his hand. It has a drawing of a pair of lungs on it. He has come to teach you something, and he has found you doing it already.*

*He folds the paper in half, and then in half again, and puts it in his pocket.*

### A bit of tooth  (y2_tooth · Misaki · injuries only · cell)

*A piece of the back tooth comes away in the soup. You find it with your tongue. It is about the size of a grain of rice.*

*You do not spit it out. A thing with a piece missing is worth less, and they knock that off somewhere. You have seen what happens to goods that arrive short.*

**Misaki:** (keep it. if he doesn’t know that it’s gone, it’s not gone)

*You hold it under your tongue through the rest of the bowl. When he comes for the tray you have it in your fist, under the blanket.*

*He looks at the fist. He looks at your jaw, where you are holding it wrong. He says that he would like to see what is in your hand, and that nothing will follow from it.*

*You open the hand. You keep your eyes on his. This is the part where you learn what short goods cost.*

*He takes the piece of tooth between finger and thumb, and holds it under the light. Then he wraps it in his handkerchief, a linen one with a letter in the corner, and puts it in his breast pocket, and goes out.*

**Misaki:** (he took it. he’s keeping it. what’s he keeping a tooth for?)

### The spoon  (y2_spoon · V · injuries only)

*They have splinted two of their own fingers. They did it with the spoon from the first tray and a strip from the hem of the sheet, torn with the teeth. The knot is on the back of the hand, where it will not press.*

*You have splinted paws. You know good work when it is in front of you.*

**V:** (It is a field dressing. It is what one does when one knows that nobody is coming.)

*They apologise to you for the sheet, and offer to pay for it.*

*You go to the surgery for a proper splint. You get as far as the cabinet, and then you are standing with both hands flat on the steel and your head down.*

**V:** (I had them for a day before I thought of the fingers. A killer with no law saw to it in an hour, with a spoon. I am disgusted, and for once I can put my finger on with whom.)

### Two out of ten  (y2_scale · V · injuries only)

*You asked them for the pain, out of ten. They considered it honestly, and said two.*

*Three bones in the hand. Three ribs. Wrists cut to the tendon sheath. A tooth broken off at the gum. Two.*

**V:** (I have had men on the table say ten at the first finger. I thought the less of them for it.)

*You know what a scale is. It is made by its top. Somewhere there is a thing that is their ten, and everything since has been measured against it and found small.*

**V:** (I do not want to know what it was. That is false. I want to know exactly, with a name and an address.)

**V:** (I have no standing to want it. I had my own entry planned for that scale. I would have been proud to reach a six.)

### They were right about the kidney  (y2_right · V · injuries only)

*They told you, as one would give the time, that the kidney had taken a boot; that pink meant bruised and not split; that it would clear in three days with water.*

*You looked it up. You have a veterinary text and a human one, and you read both.*

*They are correct on every point. They are correct to the day.*

**V:** (They have no schooling past thirteen. They did not read that. They have had it, and watched it, and counted the days, more than once.)

*You bring the water. Your hand shakes on the jug, and they see it, and you watch them wonder what they have said to upset the guard.*

**V:** (They were eleven the first time. They wrote a will. I asked what was in it, and I wish that I had not.)

### A door  (y2_door · V · injuries only)

*They gave you the tour of their own films, as one might show a visitor round a flat. The ladder. A man with a bat, whom they are proud of. A door.*

*“Somebody shut it. My arm was in it.” You asked whose door. They did not remember. Who remembers doors.*

*It is a spiral fracture of the forearm. It was never set. They were perhaps six.*

**V:** (A door does not shut on a child’s arm and stay shut by accident. Somebody leaned on it.)

*You turned the lightbox off. You have looked at the inside of a great many people without difficulty. You could not look at that one for another second.*

**V:** (I find that I am sickened by a thing done twenty years ago to somebody whom I meant to joint. There is no sense in it. I have looked for the sense.)

### The names  (y2_theirs · V · injuries only)

*You have the names of the men from the building on the river. A bald one, with a ring. A thin one, who forgot.*

*Lex talionis. A hand for a hand. You have done it a dozen times, exactly, each bone in its order.*

*You sit down to plan it, and you find that you do not want to do it in order.*

**V:** (I want to stamp on it. I want to do it badly, and at length, and for no reason, and wipe my feet afterwards. I want to do it as they did.)

*You put the pen down. You have never wanted that. You have despised the men who want that. It is the whole of the distinction on which you have stood for eleven years.*

**V:** (So it was never a distinction of nature. It was only that nobody had yet done it to something of mine.)

*You write the plan in order regardless, bone by bone. Your hand is very neat. You do not trust it.*

*You clean your instruments on a Thursday. The scalpels, the saw, the small pliers, the bone shears.*

*The shears are the ones you used on the card man. Eight fingers, one at a time, each for a finger that he took. It was good work. You stand by it.*

*You pick them up, and you see a hand in a cast, lying on a blanket, with two fingers bound to a spoon.*

**V:** (It is not the same. His were taken for cause. Theirs was broken by a fool.)

*You clean the shears and oil them and put them back in the roll. It takes you three attempts to close your hand on them, and you count the attempts, because it is the first time that has happened and you intend to know if it happens again.*

### The straps on the table  (y2_strap · V · injuries only)

*The straps on the steel table are leather, two inches wide, lined with felt. You had them made. A man held in them does not cut himself; that would be waste, and it would not be on the charge.*

*The marks on their wrists are a quarter of an inch wide and go to the tendon. Cable ties. A packet of a hundred costs less than a cup of coffee.*

**V:** (I took care over how a man should be held while I killed him. They took no care at all, and meant to let this one live.)

*You stand with one of your straps in your hand and their wrists in your head, and you cannot put the two side by side in any way that is to your credit.*

**V:** (Either the felt means that I am better than they are, or it means that I gave more thought to it. I have believed the first for eleven years. Tonight I can only find evidence for the second.)

### They have not said that it hurts  (y2_once · V · injuries only)

*You have set three bones and strapped three ribs and cleaned two wrists to the tendon. In all of it they have not once said that it hurts.*

*They have said “ow, fuck” at a cold stethoscope. They have complained at length about the soup, the pillow, your shoes and your face. They are not a quiet person.*

**V:** (They complain about everything that does not matter. About what does, they make a joke, and hold still.)

*You find that you are pressing a little, on the last strap, more than you need. You stop at once. You know what you were doing. You were trying to get one honest sound out of them.*

**V:** (That is what the men in the building did, for sport. I did it for three seconds because I could not bear how quiet they were. I have washed my hands twice.)

*You should like, once, to hear them say that it hurts. You would give a wing of the foundation for it. You will not get it by asking, and you have just learned that you must not try any other way.*

### The reasons  (y2_list · V · injuries only)

*You keep a list of the reasons. It is the first page of their file. You read it when you feel yourself slipping.*

*Kills for rent. Has no law. Did not ask what the man had done. Shot a doorman who was in the way. Sleeps well.*

*You read it tonight in the corridor, and each line has acquired a second line under it, which you did not write.*

**V:** (Kills for rent: has never had an evening that was not owed to somebody. Has no law: was never once on the right side of one. Did not ask: was never asked.)

**V:** (Sleeps well: sleeps sitting up, in a corner, in my house, and does not wake when a rib is strapped.)

*The list is still true. Every word. You fold it and put it back. It used to be the page that settled things, and now it is only a page.*
