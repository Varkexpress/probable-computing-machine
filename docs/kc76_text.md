### e2l\_show

*Three in the morning. Your phone lights up on the pillow with a link from K9 and nothing else. You open it, because you would open anything he sent you.*

*It is a camera over a door, in a room under his house: white tile, a drain in the middle of the floor, a steel table. The card man is strapped to the table in his vest. He has been there since the night of the carpet, and somebody has been feeding him.*

*V comes into frame in a rubber apron with his sleeves rolled, and holds the card man’s left hand up to the lens. It has two fingers on it. The right one has none.*

**V:** He took eight fingers from people at my shelters when they were late. I have taken eight of his, one at a time. He has two left because I have not finished. This is what the word slow means when I write it.

**V:** Your page says the same word. I wanted you to have seen it before you decide how hard to fight.

*He picks up the shears. The card man starts saying please, and he has said it so many times this week that the P has worn off it.*

*The cold room, three in the morning. The card man took eight fingers from people at your shelters, over four years, when they were late. You have taken eight of his, one at a time, and tonight he has two left.*

*He stopped offering you money on the second night. He says please now, and has said it so many times that the P has worn off it.*

**V:** (Misaki would have shot him off a roof for nine hundred dollars without asking what he had done. He would have died without knowing why.)

*There is a camera over the door. Their name is in your ledger under his, with the same word beside it, and they have never seen what the word means.*

**V:** (text) You watched to the end.

**Misaki:** (text) ur slow. i’d have been done in a second and had noodles

**V:** (text) Yes. That is the charge against you.

**Misaki:** (text) this is quick. u won’t hear it. it’s the only nice thing i’ll ever do for u

**V:** (text) I listened to him until he had nothing left to say. I would listen to you for longer.

- ▸ Watch all of it

*You watch all of it, forty minutes, with the sound on, eating cereal out of the box. He is neat and he never raises his voice. When the card man is finished, V hoses the table down himself and lets the hounds in for what is in the bucket.*

**Misaki:** (one: he means it. two: he’s done this a lot. three: i have to kill him somewhere he can’t get me down those stairs)

- ▸ Shut it. Send him his own bedroom window, through the scope

*You shut the feed, go up the water tower with the rifle, and take a photograph through the scope of his bedroom window with the crosshair on the pillow. You send it at four.*

*You do not sleep. At five the hounds go out along the wall as usual, and he walks behind them and does not look up once.*

- ▸ Send them the feed, and work

*You send the link and hold his left hand up to the lens, and tell them what the word on their page means. Then you pick up the shears. You do the ninth and the tenth without hurrying, hose the table down, and let the hounds in for the bucket.*

*The feed shows one viewer, from the first minute to the last.*

- ▸ Send them one minute of it

*You send the link, hold his left hand up to the lens, and tell them what the word on their page means. Then you cut the feed and finish alone.*

*At four they send you a photograph taken through a scope: your bedroom window, with the crosshair on the pillow.*


### e2l\_pair

*The boy with the box cutter was hired by a man who runs a minicab office behind the station, and that man knows who paid him. You go in at one in the morning, together. Neither of you would let the other go alone, and neither of you has said why.*

*(or)* *The boy with the box cutter was hired by a man who runs a minicab office behind the station, and that man knows who paid him. You go in at one in the morning, together. Neither of you would let the other go alone, and neither of you has said why.*

*It takes ninety seconds. You put out the yard camera and the dispatcher’s radio with two rounds from the pistol, and V walks in at the front and has the man face down on his own desk with a hound lead round his neck before your second casing has landed.*

*(or)* *It takes ninety seconds. They put out the yard camera and the dispatcher’s radio with two rounds, and you walk in at the front and have the man face down on his own desk with a lead round his neck before their second casing has landed.*

*You have always worked alone. Tonight he was standing where you needed him half a second before you needed it, twice.*

*(or)* *You have worked alone for eleven years. Tonight the yard light went out half a second before you needed it dark.*

**V:** He paid a child forty dollars to open a man’s throat. By my law he goes downstairs, and it takes a week.

**Misaki:** We need the name tonight, V. He says it, I put one in his head, and we go and eat.

*The man on the desk has worked out that the two of you are arguing about him. He starts to cry, and offers the name to whichever of you is kinder.*

*He says the name. You shoot him through the ear, once, and V takes his hand off the lead a second before, as though you had counted him in.*

*(or)* *He says the name. They shoot him through the ear, once, and you find you have taken your hand off the lead a second before, as though they had counted you in.*

**V:** That was wanton.

**Misaki:** That was work, V. He was done talking, and so was I.

*In the car he writes the name down. He does not enter the minicab man on your page. You watch him decide.*

*(or)* *In the car you write the name down. The minicab man belongs on their page, under the rest, and you sit with the pen over the line and do not enter him.*

*You hold the man’s head against the desk with your good hand and your knee while V works on him with the scalpel from his collar. It takes eleven minutes to get a name. You learn that V hums while he does it, and that it is the cello piece from the car.*

*(or)* *They hold the man’s head against the desk with their good hand and a knee while you work on him with the scalpel from your collar. It takes eleven minutes to get a name. They do not look away at any point; you check.*

**Misaki:** (I’m not sick. I thought I would be. I’m holding a man down for him and my hands are steady.)

**V:** (Their hands are steady. I had expected to despise them less for it, and I do, and I dislike that a great deal.)

**V:** You held him well.

*He finishes the man himself, quickly, which you did not expect, and wipes the scalpel on the man’s tie.*

*(or)* *You finish the man yourself, quickly. You tell yourself there was no time for the week. They watch you wipe the scalpel on his tie and say nothing, which is worse.*
