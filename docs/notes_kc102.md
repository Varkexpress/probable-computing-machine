## kc102: the anger, redone

Both are flawless at the work; neither has the least idea what to do with a feeling. The first version had Misaki
throw the tray, scream, bite, wreck the room and shout at Ronin. All of that is gone. The anger is now cold: a
stare held over a bowl of soup, silence for days, a private ledger on the wall, perfect manners (“Thank you, V.”),
“it’s fine” in the voice that means never, the one exact cruelty aimed at Angel's numbers. Nothing is thrown or
broken; the test checks the text for it. It does more damage than the outbursts did, and Misaki knows it while doing
it, and cannot stop.

V's side is unchanged in kind (figures, arrangements, the correct voice, a letter burned), with two scenes rewritten
to match: `s_board` (they answer his board-meeting voice in it, perfectly: “thank u, V. that’s very clear”) and
`s_bitten`, now “They have stopped speaking” (he reads them the weather to fill it; “Day 3. Nothing said.”).

## kc103: Oh

Linked to kc102: Misaki has frozen everybody out, as they have since they were nine, and it has always worked:
people go. This time nobody goes. Misaki adds it up, as they add everything up, and the sum comes out to a thing they
have no column for. It is a realisation, not a romance; Angel and Ronin are in it as much as V.

A free ⚔ side entry, one a day, ordered, after the infirmary and before the boat, once kc102's anger has begun (any
of its clear infirmary scenes, or the range morning, which comes by itself).

Misaki (`S.f.e2l103M`):
1. Ronin, at four: nineteen days running. “’S four o’clock.”
2. Angel's other face: she put on her camera face in the toilet so that they would not have to see her sad; they
   watch ninety seconds of her stream from the night they were taken.
3. His ledger: “Nothing.”, “Day 3. Nothing said.”, and the deed to a plot under a yew, bought at three in the
   morning. They write on the back of it (`S.f.e2l103Deed`).
4. Count again: on a roof, nine fingers, where the infirmary made it four.
5. Half past seven, a Wednesday: a cup of coffee the way they take it. “(oh)” “(oh, FUCK)” “ur coffee’s shit.” “So I
   am told.” (`S.f.e2l103Oh`). Their hands shake, and they let them.

V (`S.f.e2l103V`): Ronin in the passage (“I have told them that the stitches come out on the fourteenth.”); the
first “ur honour” after a month of “Thank you, V” (he burns the bacon); the deed, refolded: “not yet. —M.” and
“north wall’s nice tho. good tree.” His third waits until the deed has been found.
