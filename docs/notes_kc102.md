# What they are bad at (kc102)

`src/kc102.js`; tests `tools/k102_test.js` (48 checks) and `tools/k86_test.js` (the pool); reading copy
`docs/kc102_text.md`. Needs kc73 and kc76–kc101. About 5,000 words.

## The direction (author)

“further expand on this, and make it clearer neither of them are perfect (or, they are and they both must be, in
everything but emotionally) and give misaki more anger”

## The idea

They are perfect at the work, because in their work one mistake is the end: eleven stitches you could rule a line
along; six hundred metres in the rain with one round. Neither has the first idea what to do with a feeling. V makes
arrangements, quotes a figure, writes it down and burns it, goes correct when frightened, and leaves the room. Misaki
throws things, picks the fight, throws out anybody who is kind, and will not be helped. Each fails the other at the
exact point where the other needs it, knows it at the time, and does it again.

Misaki's anger is real and loud and often unfair, and it goes at the wrong people as well as the right ones.

## The infirmary (fourteen pool scenes; the pool is 195)

Misaki, during the delusion (anger at the man they believe is holding them; the standing rule holds):
- `s_tray` The tray, thrown. He cleans the soup off the wall on his knees, and that is worse.
- `s_scream` An hour of screaming at the door until the voice goes. He was sitting outside the whole time.
- `s_bite` They bite his thumb to the bone. He finishes the dressing first.

Misaki, once clear:
- `s_wreck` They wreck the room. He stands in the door and says that he will have the glass replaced.
- `s_ronin` They scream at Ronin for being two streets over, knowing it is unfair. Neither can say sorry.
- `s_debt` “u had no RIGHT.” He enters the river on their page as a credit, to be kind. “it’s not a fucking LINE.”
- `s_four` Plans for the four, then a fifth: the one who walked past the van.
- `s_pity` They throw Angel out for her pity face. She comes back with her camera face on, for them.

V:
- `s_figure` “am i gonna be ok” gets a recovery percentage.
- `s_letter` A perfect letter, three drafts, burned.
- `s_wrong` They cry; he knows how to quiet a hound and cannot use it; he leaves, knowing it is wrong.
- `s_board` The board-meeting voice. “talk to me like a fucking PERSON.” He cannot find the other voice.
- `s_bitten` The bite, from his side. “Bit me. Right thumb. Good.”
- `s_steady` His hands never shake. Nothing else comes out either.

## Mornings (shared cap, after the route's other mornings)

- `m102_range` (after the infirmary) Two hundred rounds of rage on his range. He cannot stop them or help them; he
  puts a fifth box on the bench.
- `m102_job` (Act II) A perfect job, and twenty minutes of fighting over the car radio because neither can say
  “good shot.”
- `m102_sorry` (after the corridor) Misaki shoots the stone ball off his gatepost; V writes “Owed: an apology. By me.”
  in the ledger, and has a second ball carved for the post. Neither understands the other.

## The worst route

Two more daily scenes in kc97's set (fray and thin), both about anger: holding on to it as the thing that gets you
up, and then missing it “the way you would miss a dog”.
