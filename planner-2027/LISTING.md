## Title

## Description
Start 2027 organized. A clean, minimalist **hyperlinked digital planner** for iPad and Android tablets.

**What's inside (102 pages with links throughout):**
- Year at a glance: tap any date to jump to its week
- 2027 goals page (health, money, relationships, growth)
- 12 monthly calendars with focus and notes panels
- 53 weekly spreads (weeks start Monday) with a priorities checklist and previous/next links
- 12 monthly habit trackers (16 habits each)
- 12 monthly budget pages (income, bills, spending)
- 10 notes pages
- Side tabs on every page: Year, Goals, Jan–Dec, Habits, Budget, Notes

**3 colorways included:** Sage, Blush, Slate.

**Works with:** GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf, Xodo, Samsung Notes, and any PDF app that supports links. You can also print it.

**How it works:** Download it right away, open the PDF in your note app and write with your stylus. This is a digital product, so nothing ships.

## Rebuild or add colors
Run `python3 build.py sage|blush|slate`. To add a colorway, add it to `THEMES` in build.py. To make a 2028 edition, change `YEAR`.
