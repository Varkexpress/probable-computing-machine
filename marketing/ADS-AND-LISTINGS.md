## Ads and organic posts (free first, paid second)

### Pinterest (best free channel for printables)
Upload `out/pin-*.png` (1000×1500). Post 1–2 pins a day and link every pin to the product page.
- Pin title: *Free time saver: 30 printable anime coloring pages for kids*
- Pin description: *Bold, original chibi characters kids love. Print at home in seconds, perfect for road trips, rainy days and classrooms. #kidscoloringpages #printablecoloring #animecoloring #kawaii #kidsactivities #homeschool*
- Boards to create: "Printable Coloring Pages for Kids", "Kawaii Coloring", "Rainy Day Kids Activities", "Christmas Activities for Kids", "Digital Planners 2027".

### TikTok, Reels and Shorts (15 seconds, no face needed)
1. Hook text: *"POV: you found 90 coloring pages for less than a latte ☕"*
2. Scroll through the pages, then show one page being colored (a printout and crayons, sped up).
3. End card: the logo and *"link in bio"*.
Sound: any trending upbeat track. Hashtags: #coloringpages #momhack #kidsactivities #printables #kawaii

### Facebook and Instagram (paid, once the first organic sale proves the listing converts)
- Budget: $5/day for 5 days, Advantage+ audience. Interests: parents of 3–8 year olds, homeschooling, coloring books.
- Creative: `out/pin-bundle.png` (feed) and `out/thumb-bundle.png` (square).
- Primary text: *Screen-free fun in 30 seconds ⏱️ Print, color, repeat. 90 original kids' coloring pages (anime chibis, kawaii critters, magic patterns) for $9.99. Instant download.*
- Headline: *90 Coloring Pages · $9.99*. Button: Shop Now.
- Stop the ad if no sales after $25. If it sells, raise the budget 20% every 3 days.

### Reddit (follow each sub's self-promo rules)
- r/printables, r/coloring, r/Parenting (weekly self-promo thread), r/digitalplanner (planner).
- Post title: *I made a free sample page from my kids' anime-style coloring book, feedback welcome!* Share one free page and put the shop link in a comment.

### Free sample = email list
Offer one page free (for example "Kai the Cat Whisperer") in exchange for an email address. Shopify Forms makes this easy. Email the list when a new book drops.

### Seasonal pushes (now → Dec 20)
Christmas and stocking-stuffer printables peak in the first three weeks of December. Pin "stocking stuffer" and "Christmas break activities" angles now.
