## Title

**Ribcage: Homes on the Ice**

## Description

Four impossible concrete homes stand alone in a frozen southern sea, and one of them is yours.

🦴 **THE RIBCAGE**: a 560-stud concrete spine arching out of the water, ribs hanging over the ice, with a glass gallery in its crown. Lap pool, glass floor over a 200-stud drop, and a fire bowl hanging from the ceiling.
🗿 **THE MONOLITH**: a 220-stud slab with a single slit of glass. Three stacked floors and a sunken fire pit.
🦴 **THE VERTEBRAE**: five concrete cubes on pylons, stepping up out of the sea, joined by glass bridges.
🕳️ **THE WELL**: a concrete cylinder open to the sky, built around a black pool, with a spiral ramp and a single hanging cube of light.

**LIVE THERE**
• Claim a home and wake up in it every day
• Cook 8 dishes, from berry pancakes to fisherman's stew, in a timing mini-game, then sit at the table and eat them (sitting down gives a bonus)
• Keep warm: fires burn down unless you add logs, and the cold outside is real
• A daily routine: coffee, dishes, plants, making the bed, reading by the fire
• Mochi the house cat naps in warm spots. Adopt a kitten, Samoyed puppy, snow rabbit or a little penguin that follows you

**THE NIGHT THE SEA FREEZES**
Every night the sea turns to ice and you can walk between the homes. At dawn it cracks, so don't get caught out there.

**A SECRET UNDER THE ARCH**
The architect left notes hidden in all four homes. One of them is the tuning of the ribs. Play it on the rib organ and something unlocks far below the arch…

Also: drive boats between docks, ride the ferry, fish for dinner, sit out blizzards, watch for whales in the fog, take screenshots in photo mode (P), and buy upgrades like a heated pool and the southern lights.
